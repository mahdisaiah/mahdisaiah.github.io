What 3D taught me about interfaces.

Every image starts as a simple block-out, then gets built up layer by layer: clean modelling, physical materials, lighting treated like a film set, and a frame that says something.

It's the same work as on a screen: hierarchy, light that guides the eye, what you choose not to show. It's also what helped me design 3D visualisation interfaces at BNP Paribas Real Estate.
